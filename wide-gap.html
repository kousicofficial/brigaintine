WIDE GAP

Wide Gap
Heat Exchangers

Engineered for fibrous fluids and coarse particles, the Wide Gap gasketed plate-and-frame heat exchangers prevent clogging while maximizing efficiency.

Product Overview

Engineered for fibrous fluids and coarse particles, the Wide Gap gasketed plate-and-frame heat exchangers prevent clogging while maximizing efficiency. Ideal for processing sugar, bioethanol, and pulp and paper.

With its flexible wide-narrow channel configurations, this unit guarantees a smooth and uninterrupted flow even for highly suspended solids, ensuring continuous factory operation.

BRIGANTINE WIDE GAP

Key Features

Uninterrupted Fluid Flow

Reduced risk of blockage specifically for fluids containing coarse particles and slurries.

Flexible Geometry

Can be configured in wide-wide or wide-narrow channel options depending on specific duties.

Versatile Usage

Functions efficiently as a heater, cooler, or interchanger for various challenging mediums.

Technology Highlights

  • Wider Gasket Gaps for Coarse Passage
  • Advanced Plate Profile Limits Clogging
  • Easily Serviced and Cleaned via CIP
  • Excellent Resistance to Abrasive Media

Benefits

Achieve enhanced operational outcomes with reliable efficiency:

  • Maximum uptime in heavy fouling applications
  • Energy-efficient, significantly lowering operating costs
  • Adjustable heat transfer area for future-proofing
  • Compact footprint allowing straightforward installation

Applications

Wastewater Treatment

Ensures continuous heat exchange in systems dealing with heavily laden effluents and sludge.

Food & Beverage

Reliable temperature control for processes involving fruit juices, purees, or dense syrups.

Pulp & Paper

Perfect for heat recovery in processes laden with long fibers and harsh residues.

Bioethanol Production

Uninterrupted cooling and heating for distillation phases with thick fermentation meshes.
