// Service Overview

Precision Electrical Diagnostics for Industrial Equipment

Electrical faults in industrial processing equipment can cascade into costly production losses. Brigantine Engineering's electrical service team delivers structured fault-finding, repair, and system restoration for motors, drives, control systems, and electrical assemblies.

From a tripped motor starter to a complex variable speed drive fault, we apply a methodical diagnostic process, OEM documentation, and precision test equipment to identify and resolve electrical issues quickly and accurately.

  • Fault diagnosis across motors, drives, and control systems
  • Repair of separator, decanter, and heat exchanger electrical systems
  • Site-safe practices compliant with Australian standards
  • Complete electrical test and certification documentation

// Technical Scope

Core Service Capabilities

Our electrical service capability spans the full electrical architecture of industrial centrifuges, separators, and heat exchanger plant.

01
Motor Fault Diagnosis

Insulation resistance testing, winding continuity checks, and field repair or rewind coordination for industrial electric motors.

02
VSD Troubleshooting

Diagnosis of overcurrent, overvoltage, and thermal trips. Parameter resetting and commissioning post-repair across major VSD brands.

03
PLC Fault Finding

Systematic panel inspection, relay testing, and PLC input/output diagnostics for industrial process control systems.

04
Instrument Calibration

Testing and replacement of process instruments including pressure transmitters, flow meters, and vibration probes.

05
Safety System Verification

Testing of emergency stops, overspeed protection, and safety interlocks for full compliance with Australian standards.

06
System Commissioning

Full system electrical commissioning following repair. Load testing and sequence verification before operational sign-off.
