Product Overview

The CB line of copper-brazed plate heat exchangers combines precision with a remarkably compact footprint, designed specifically for tight installations without compromising on thermal impact.

Utilizing advanced copper-brazing technology, these units are hermetically sealed under high temperature, ensuring excellent thermal conductivity, immense durability, and long-term energy savings.

BRIGANTINE BRAZED PHE

Key Features

Advanced Copper-Brazing

Provides optimal sealing and longevity, removing the need for traditional gaskets.

FlexFlow Plate Technology

Enables highly efficient thermal performance adaptable to various refrigerants and process liquids.

Ice Safe Feature

Patented protection against crystal formation for high-stress condensation and defrosting duties.

Technology Highlights

  • Asymmetric Channels for Lower Pressure Drop
  • Gasket-Free Hermetic Copper Seal
  • High Mechanical Resistance
  • Optimized for High-Pressure Duties

Benefits

Achieve enhanced operational outcomes with reliable efficiency:

  • Extremely compact, optimizing limited space
  • Extended equipment lifespan with minimal maintenance
  • High pressure efficiency handling up to 90 bar
  • Decreased lifecycle costs through energy recovery

Applications

HVAC Systems

Exceptional for chillers, heat pumps, and district heating/cooling substations.

Industrial Refrigeration

Effectively acts as evaporators, condensers, and economizers in modern cooling loops.

Process Oil Cooling

Efficiently cools down lubrication and hydraulic oils in heavy industrial machinery.

Domestic Hot Water

Provides instant and safe heating for sanitary water systems in residential complexes.
